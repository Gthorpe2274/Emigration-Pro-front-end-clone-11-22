# Memory

## Active Projects
| Name | What |
|------|------|
| **Emigration Pro** | Sales funnel for personalized, city-level emigration reports for U.S. citizens planning to relocate abroad. |

## Product Quick Reference
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Free Assessment | Three-step, roughly five-minute questionnaire covering personal profile, destination preferences, and seven priorities. |
| Results Page | Shows destination/city, compatibility score, budget fit, report offer, and a link to the free Relocation Hub. |
| Relocation Hub | Temporary personalized resource page with providers, videos, communities, and getting-started tips; report buyers receive two years of access with quarterly updates. |
| Full Report | Personalized, city-level professional report generated on demand with no waiting period; includes 14 research sections and is advertised at $69.99 (from $99.99). |

→ Full product knowledge: memory/projects/emigration-pro-product-knowledge.md

## Preferences
- Build sales messaging around the funnel: free assessment → personalized proof → free Hub → professional report.
- Lead with the complete stack: personalized assessment and result, city-level intelligence, on-demand report, 14 sections, timeline, and Relocation Hub assets.
- Position the report as personalized and available on demand with no wait.
