# Emigration Pro Product Knowledge

**Site reviewed:** https://emigrationpro.com  
**Review date:** August 7, 2026  
**Test scenario:** Fictional 42-year-old remote marketing consultant, one nine-year-old child, $2,500 monthly housing budget, targeting Porto, Portugal.

## Core Positioning

Emigration Pro is aimed at U.S. citizens seriously planning to relocate abroad. Its key differentiator is city-level research: the message is that customers move to a city, not merely a country. The product converts broad relocation interest into a personalized action plan.

## Funnel Observed

1. A free, no-credit-card assessment promises results in about five minutes.
2. The three assessment stages collect personal details, destination and city preferences, climate/location preferences, and priority ratings.
3. The results page provides a compatibility score, plain-language match rating, budget analysis, customer profile summary, and two next steps: buy the report or visit the free Relocation Hub.
4. The personalized Hub demonstrates the depth and practical value of the paid product.
5. The personalized report is generated on demand with no waiting period, is offered at $69.99 reduced from $99.99, and includes two years of Hub access with quarterly updates.

## Assessment Inputs

- Age, occupation, monthly housing budget, number and ages of children, and education needs.
- Preferred country and city, climate, and city/beachside/rural setting.
- Seven rated priorities: immigration policies, healthcare quality, safety and security, high-speed internet, U.S. emigration process, ease of immigration, and local acceptance.

## Example Result Observed

- Porto, Portugal received a 100/100 compatibility score when every priority was rated 5/5.
- Budget status was “Excellent.” The page said a $2,500 monthly budget (€2,300) comfortably covered premium one- to two-bedroom rentals and displayed example ranges of €500–€900 for a one-bedroom and €700–€1,300 for a two-bedroom.
- The result personalized the offer around immigration requirements, healthcare transition planning, cost-of-living analysis, and family relocation steps.

The example figures are site-generated and should be treated as marketing/demo output, not guaranteed current market data.

## Relocation Hub

The free Hub is personalized to the customer’s selected country and city. Temporary access lasts until the browser tab is closed. Buying the full report adds two years of access with quarterly updates.

Hub content observed:

- Professional service categories and providers: immigration law (Fragomen), residence/citizenship planning (Henley & Partners), international finance (Wise), global health insurance (Cigna Global), and moving/relocation services (SIRVA).
- Directories for U.S. visa application locations and customs brokers.
- Video-resource topics: American expat experience, cost-of-living comparison, immigration process, healthcare, cultural differences, and Porto neighborhoods.
- Community pathways: Facebook groups, Reddit communities, and Discord servers.
- Getting-started guidance: research visas, join expat communities, consult qualified professionals, plan finances, and consider a reconnaissance trip.
- Repeated disclaimer that peer resources and general guidance do not replace qualified immigration, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

## Strongest Sales Messages

- City-level answers instead of generic country overviews.
- A personalized plan based on family, work, budget, and priorities.
- Fourteen important research areas organized in one deliverable.
- Less uncertainty and less fragmented DIY research.
- A free assessment and free Hub make the paid recommendation feel earned.
- Two years of Hub access and quarterly updates add continuity after purchase.
- On-demand generation provides an immediate, concrete outcome without a waiting period.

## Trust and Compliance Notes

- Do not promise visa approval, eligibility, legal outcomes, savings, or a successful move.
- Present compatibility scores as assessment outputs, not guarantees.
- Avoid treating demo housing figures or provider descriptions as independently verified facts without fresh validation.
- Keep the site’s guidance disclaimer visible near purchase CTAs.
