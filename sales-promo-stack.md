**2. Personalized compatibility result**

**3. City-level intelligence**  
The experience is organized around the actual city—not broad country averages. It addresses local housing costs, healthcare access, schools, transportation, internet, infrastructure, safety, culture, recreation, and daily-life considerations.

**4. Personalized on-demand report**  
The professional report is generated for the individual customer, their family situation, priorities, budget, destination, and selected city. It is available on demand, so the customer does not have to wait for a generic report to be manually prepared.

- Two years of Hub access with quarterly updates for report purchasers

The report connects research to execution through departure steps, a master relocation timeline, and clear areas to confirm with qualified professionals.

## FAQ / Objection Handling

**Why pay when information is available online?**  
The value goes beyond the 14 sections. Customers receive a personalized assessment result, city-level intelligence, budget analysis, an on-demand report, a structured timeline, and a Relocation Hub containing professional, video, and community resources.

**Does the report guarantee I can move?**  
No. It provides general guidance and planning support. Visa eligibility and other official decisions must be confirmed with government authorities and qualified professionals.

**Is this only for retirees?**  
No. The assessment collects occupation, budget, family, education needs, and lifestyle priorities, making the funnel relevant to workers, remote professionals, families, and retirees.

**What happens after the free assessment?**  
You receive a compatibility result and budget analysis, then access a personalized Relocation Hub. Buying the professional report adds the complete 14-section city-level deliverable and two years of Hub access with quarterly updates.

**How quickly is the report delivered?**  
The report is personalized and generated on demand, with no waiting period.
